# Privacy Policy — Saha

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Saha (“the app”) helps you find publicly available Hindu sloka and mantra text, transliterate it, generate an English meaning, and export a PDF. This policy describes what we collect and why.

## Who we are

Saha is operated by the publisher listed on the App Store listing. Contact: the support email published with the App Store record.

## Information we process

- **Search queries.** When you search for a sloka, the text you type is sent to our backend.
- **Source URLs.** Candidate page URLs from search are fetched by our server to extract verse text.
- **Page text.** Up to a bounded excerpt of the fetched page, together with your search name, may be sent to an AI language-model provider so it can extract verses.
- **Verse text.** If you tap Show meaning, the extracted verses are sent to an AI language-model provider to generate an English meaning.
- **Device-only data.** Recent searches, saved slokas, exported PDFs, and your data-sharing permission stay on your device. We do not run user accounts in version 1.
- **Technical logs.** Our host may record IP address, request time, and status code for security and rate limiting. Logs are kept only as long as needed to operate the service.

We do **not** collect your name, email, or precise location. We do **not** sell personal information. We do **not** use advertising SDKs.

## Third-party processors

Data leaves our server as follows:

- The **search query** is sent to a web search provider (Brave Search or Google Programmable Search, depending on configuration).
- The **page URL** is requested from the third-party website that published it.
- **Page text plus the search query** may be sent to an AI language-model provider (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google) to extract verses.
- **Verse text** may be sent to an AI language-model provider to generate a meaning.
- **IP address and request metadata** may be recorded by our hosting provider (for example Render or Fly.io).

Those processors are required to protect the data at least as well as this policy. They process the request under their own terms as well.

## Legal basis / purpose

We process the data above to provide the search, extraction, transliteration, meaning, and PDF features you request, to keep the service secure, and to comply with law. We ask for explicit in-app permission before the first search or meaning request.

## Retention

On-device history, saved items, and consent remain until you clear them or uninstall the app. Server caches of extracted pages and generated meanings may be stored for a limited time to reduce cost and latency, keyed by URL or text hash, not by user identity.

## Your choices

Before the first search (and before Show meaning if you have not already agreed), the app shows a disclosure sheet. You must tap **Continue** before we send your query or verse/page text to third parties, including third-party AI. **Not now** cancels that action. You can withdraw permission on the Search screen; new searches and meanings will not run until you agree again. You can delete local history and saved items in the app, or by uninstalling. Because we do not create accounts, there is no cloud profile to delete.

## Children

Saha is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We will update this page if our practices change. The “Last updated” date will change accordingly.

## Contact

Use the support email on the App Store listing.
